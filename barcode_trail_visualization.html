How the flow works now:

1. Raw Materials are pulled from stock.

2. During the Pipeline, if any goods are defective, the user logs scrap. The backend dynamically reads the pipeline's scrapRouting setting and generates a new LOT-SCRAP barcode, automatically placing it in that specific inventory subgroup.

3. When the pipeline Completes, the backend calculates the final output and securely generates a Fresh Barcode (LOT-RUN), inserting it directly into your available inventory.

4. Finally, when creating a Delivery Challan, the system easily finds this fresh LOT-RUN barcode in stock and successfully deducts it for dispatch.
