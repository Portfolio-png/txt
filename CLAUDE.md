# Paper

## Skills

- **Units.** For anything involving quantities or units — reading, storing, parsing,
  converting, summing, comparing or displaying a weight, count, length, area, volume
  or time, touching the units master, or adding a column/field/cell that shows a
  quantity — follow `.claude/skills/units/SKILL.md`. Read its §0 first: it records
  what is actually built versus what the contract targets, and most of the module it
  describes does not exist yet.
- **Design Philosophy.** For core architectural principles, multi-client screen adaptation,
  canonical data modeling, backend invariant enforcement, shop-floor event logging, and
  feature-to-metric clustering — follow `.claude/skills/design-philosophy/SKILL.md` (and
  `Docs/architecture/10-design-philosophy.md`). Run its §6 pre-implementation checklist
  before building any feature or client-specific screen.

## Architecture notes

`Docs/architecture/` holds the reasoning behind the larger pieces. `07` is the vision
and open backlog; `08` is the master link graph; `09` is variation trees and versions;
`10` is design philosophy and multi-client architecture.
